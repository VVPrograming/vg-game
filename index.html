Slide. Merge. Reach 2048.

A number puzzle for your phone. Swipe to slide the tiles, join equal numbers, and keep going as high as you can.

Download APK Play in browser

Free for Android. Works offline.

How to play

  1. Swipe up, down, left or right. Every tile slides as far as it can.
  2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one tile with their sum.
  3. Reach 2048, then keep playing. The game ends only when no move is left.

What is inside

  • 4 themesIndigo, Ocean, Sunset and Dark.
  • Sound effectsSoft tones you can switch off.
  • Saved progressYour board and best score stay on your phone.
  • Works offlineNo internet needed after install.
  • Private lockProtect the game with a PIN or a pattern.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download APK and wait for vg-game.apk to finish downloading.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, choose Install anyway.
  4. Open VG game from your home screen and rotate to landscape.

Prefer no download? Tap Play in browser, then choose Install app in Chrome's menu.
